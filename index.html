The arithmetic

Year by year

Every starting figure is taken from the City of Clarksville Fiscal Year 2027 budget book, from the columns headed “FY2027 Proposed” in the Mayor’s transmitted budget of May 1, 2026: a $8.06B assessed tax base, $81,436,513 of property tax at the adopted $1.01 rate, $34,168,124 of local option sales tax, $189,435,257 of total revenue, and $187,894,893 of total expenditures against $153,586,035 of department appropriations. Each department is split into its salary-and-wage accounts and its operating accounts exactly as the budget book reports them.

Property tax revenue grows only with the tax base, because Tennessee’s certified tax rate law makes reappraisal revenue-neutral, so rising home values alone never appear here as new money. Debt service, transit, and the shared and agency obligations are shown as fixed, because they are: they are contractual or intergovernmental and a council cannot cut them in a budget session. Rates are dollars per $100 of assessed value.
